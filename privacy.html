Privacy Policy

Jairoz Studios · Effective September 26, 2026

This document has not been reviewed by outside legal counsel. Jairoz Studios is an early-stage, solo-operated studio; this policy describes our current practice in plain language and will be formalized with legal review as the studio grows.

1. Who we are

Jairoz Studios is an independent entertainment studio, operated by an individual (Jairo Alonso Quiroz Campos). Contact: hello@jairoz.com.

2. What we collect

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. We do not operate a public form that collects visitor data. If you email us directly, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that email, and use it only to reply to you.

3. Platform API access

Jairoz Studios connects to the YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, and TikTok Content Posting API using OAuth credentials tied to our own official accounts (@jairozstudios). This access is used only to publish and manage our own content. We do not request, store, or process data belonging to any other user of those platforms, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

4. Data we store

The only data our internal tools store are: (a) the OAuth access and refresh tokens for our own official accounts, kept in a private, access-controlled location and never committed to a public repository; and (b) drafts and metadata (titles, descriptions, tags) for our own video content prior to publishing. None of this is shared with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Jairoz Studios' content is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change materially, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to review or delete any data we hold about you, can be sent to hello@jairoz.com.
